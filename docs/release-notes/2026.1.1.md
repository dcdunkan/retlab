> ##### TL;DR
>
> Retlab is live!

Etlab is one of the most unnecessarily overengineered complex applications that I know of, presented through even worser user client apps.
I started hating everything about it the very first time I used, in the first week of my first semester itself.
I decided to build an alternative to the whole Etlab that day; but I never got to do that, because PLANS NEVER WORK.

Time passed. Roughly 3 more semesters to go.

But, in that limited time, I could still do something to make my life easier.
May be not a full replacement? Just try to present the data well? Make it convenient?

So, Retlab is an attempt I made at building an alternative frontend for Etlab.
It uses the same APIs Etlab native apps use, and designed to be lightweight & secure.
Not some "vybe"-coded shitty application.
And I hope to fill it with cool features, as it is more of a toy project.

That's all & I hope you find Ret useful. Thank you so much.
